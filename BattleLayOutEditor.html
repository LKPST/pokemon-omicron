Battle Layout Editor

Drag the Pokémon, trainers, platforms, HP boxes, party balls, text box and buttons around the real battle screen. It reads PokemonDatabase.js, TrainerTables.js, BattleEngine.js and BattleScene.js straight from your project, and gives you back a new PokemonDatabase.js (per-Pokémon X/Y) and a new BattleScene.js (everything else). Keep this page in your project root and open it from the preview URL so the sprites load.
 
Click an element to select it, drag to move it. Arrow keys nudge the selected one by 1 px (Shift = 5 px). Units are GBA pixels (top screen 240×155).

How the positions work
  • Pokémon: the layout block has the default spot for every species (Anchor X = middle of the sprite, Feet Y = where it stands). The database fields xPosFront / yPosFront (opponent) and xPosBack / yPosBack (yours) shift one species from that default (503,107 and 129,359 = no shift).
  • Dragging a Pokémon changes that species in the database. Hold Shift (or switch the drop-down) to move every species at once.
  • Trainers, platforms, HP boxes, party balls, the level-up box, the text box and the four buttons are saved in the BattleScene.CONFIG block of BattleScene.js.
  • Party balls are placed relative to their HP box, so moving the box takes them along.
